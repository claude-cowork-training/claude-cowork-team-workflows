# 15. Case Study First Draft

**Category:** Marketing · **Time saved:** ~3 hrs per piece (estimate — based on replacing the blank-page phase of case-study writing; interviews and approval still take their usual time) · **Difficulty:** ●●○

**What it does:** Assembles a full case-study first draft from an engagement's real artifacts — scope doc, delivery notes, outcome summary — in the classic problem/approach/result structure, with every claim sourced and every gap flagged for the interview. You start editing at 70%, not writing at 0%.

**You need:** A per-client engagement folder (scope, notes, outcome data), your style guide, and one past case study as the structure anchor.

## The prompt

```
Read everything in clients/[CLIENT]/, team-context/style-guide.md,
and the example in marketing/case-studies/example.md.
Draft a case study in the example's structure:
1. The problem — in the client's terms, from the scope and kickoff
   notes.
2. What we did — concrete, chronological, no methodology padding.
3. Results — ONLY figures present in the folder; client-stated
   outcomes are labeled "client-reported". Every number cites its
   source file.
4. Pull quote placeholders: "[QUOTE: ask about X]" wherever a
   client voice would land — with the specific question to ask.
Flag every gap as "[INTERVIEW: ...]". Do not fill gaps with
plausible details.
Save as marketing/case-studies/drafts/[CLIENT]-case-study-v1.md.
```

## Setup (one time)

1. Keep engagement artifacts in one per-client folder (workflows 04 and 10 already build this).
2. Add your best past case study as the example.

## Running it (each time)

1. Run when an engagement hits a milestone; the `[INTERVIEW]` and `[QUOTE]` flags become your client-call agenda.
2. Client approval before publication, always — the draft is internal until then.

## Adapt it

- **One-page version:** add "also produce a one-page summary for sales to attach to proposals".
- **Anonymous version:** add "a second draft with the client de-identified ('a mid-size manufacturer')".
- **Award submission:** re-cut to a specific entry format by swapping the example file.

## Common failure + fix

**Failure:** Invented texture — plausible details about the client's process that no source file supports, which the client catches at approval (embarrassing) or doesn't (worse).
**Fix:** The no-plausible-details rule plus per-number citations. Tested on the Metamorfeus Ferrostar folder: the draft carried the ~1,400→~120 alerts figure with its "client-reported" label and generated interview flags instead of filler for the unsourced parts.
